Procedure

  1. Click on the Component button to place components on the table.
  2. Make connections as per the circuit diagram and pin diagram of IC or according to the connection table.
  3. Click on the Check Connections button. If connections are correct, click on 'OK'; the simulation will become active.
  4. Provide the input by clicking toggle switches A0, A1, A2, and A3.
  5. Fill the observed values in the Truth Table.
  6. Verify the Truth Table by clicking on the Check button. If outputs are correct, click on 'OK'.
  7. Click on the Reset button to reset the page.

Components

  • Breadboard
  • 5V DC Supply
  • LEDs (L0–L9)
  • IC 7442
  • Switches
  • 1kΩ Resistors (R0–R9)
